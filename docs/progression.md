# Progression Shape

This is an early progression sketch. It should guide mod selection, quest writing, and config decisions.

## Act 1: Signal Overload

The player starts with tech-forward tools and a feeling of pressure. Resource generation is possible, but the systems should feel busy, layered, and slightly too demanding.

Gameplay ideas:

- basic power and machines
- early automation
- storage pressure
- resource processing chains
- quests framed around coping, masking, and keeping everything running

Narrative feeling:

> I can do this, but why does everything take so much from me?

## Act 2: Naming The Pattern

The player begins to understand the machinery around them. Some systems become more efficient, but the quests start asking whether efficiency is the same as wellbeing.

Gameplay ideas:

- sorting and simplifying automation
- replacing noisy loops with cleaner ones
- unlocking renewable resources
- first nature-centered production paths
- base areas that feel less industrial

Narrative feeling:

> The system is not me. I can change the system.

## Act 3: Sustainable Sky

The pack opens into calmer, slower, renewable play. Nature-based resource gathering becomes viable and eventually preferable for many materials.

Gameplay ideas:

- trees, farms, gardens, bees, mobs, or other renewable resource systems depending on final Fabric mod availability
- lower-stress crafting chains
- decorative and comfort-focused progression
- rituals of rest, repair, and self-trust

Narrative feeling:

> I am allowed to build a life that fits me.

## Modlist Direction

The final modlist is still open. The desired arc is:

1. Start with tech and automation as the first major resource language.
2. Introduce tools that reduce friction and cognitive load.
3. Gradually unlock nature-based resource systems.
4. Let the endgame feel calm, renewable, and emotionally resolved instead of endlessly extractive.
