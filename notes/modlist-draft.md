# Modlist Draft Notes

No final modlist has been chosen yet.

Current installed profile mods are mostly foundation, performance, UI, and shader support. Future mod choices should be evaluated by how well they serve the story:

- Does this mod add early automation, pressure, or system complexity?
- Can that complexity be simplified or healed later?
- Does this mod support sustainable or nature-based resource gathering?
- Does it make the skyblock experience feel more personal, sensory-kind, or story-rich?

## Possible Categories

- Fabric skyblock/world-start tooling
- Questing and narrative delivery
- Early tech and automation
- Storage and logistics
- Renewable/nature resource generation
- Farming, trees, bees, or ecology systems
- Decoration and cozy base-building
- Accessibility, UI, and performance support
