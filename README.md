# New Sky 1010: New Beginnings

**New Sky 1010: New Beginnings** is a story-driven Fabric Minecraft modpack about life after a late autism diagnosis: the shock of finally having a name for your patterns, the chaos of rebuilding your systems, and the slow movement toward a life that feels more sustainable, sensory-kind, and real.

The working title is rooted in the spiritual and numerological meaning of **1010**. In many numerology spaces, 1010 is read as a sign of new beginnings, alignment, awakening, and stepping through a threshold with more trust in yourself. In this pack, that number becomes a skyblock metaphor: you begin suspended in the raw static of change, surrounded by machines, pressure, and overbuilt coping systems. Progression slowly asks you to trade frantic automation for calmer rhythms: renewable resources, nature-based gathering, intentional craft, and a home that supports you instead of consuming you.

This pack is a sibling project to [AuADHD; a skyward journey into the void](https://github.com/AuTISTICaRTIST87/AuADHD-a-skyward-journey-into-the-void), but it is not meant to repeat the same arc. **New Sky 1010** focuses more directly on the post-diagnosis period: what happens after the reveal, when life does not magically become easy, but the map finally starts making sense.

## GitHub Description

Fabric skyblock modpack about late autism diagnosis, 1010 as new beginnings, and evolving from chaotic tech automation into calmer nature-based resource systems.

## Core Theme

At the beginning, the player is not broken. They are overloaded.

Early gameplay should feel capable but noisy: tech, automation, storage, power, task pressure, and too many systems demanding attention at once. That phase represents the immediate aftermath of late diagnosis, where old coping structures are still running even as the player begins to understand why they were built.

As the story progresses, the pack should gently shift toward:

- sustainable resource loops
- nature-based production
- lower-pressure crafting goals
- sensory-soft worldbuilding
- rebuilding identity after masking
- choosing systems that care for the player instead of merely extracting output

## Design Direction

- **Loader:** Fabric
- **Minecraft version:** To be finalized from the Modrinth profile
- **Pack structure:** Story-driven skyblock progression
- **Opening mood:** Too much tech, too much automation, too many systems
- **Long-term mood:** Calm, sustainable, nature-centered, self-trusting
- **Narrative lens:** Late autism diagnosis, masking, burnout, identity reconstruction, and learning to live from truth instead of survival mode

## Status

This repository is in early concept and profile setup. A final modlist has not been chosen yet. The first design pass is focused on defining the emotional arc, progression philosophy, and the kinds of mods that can support the story.
